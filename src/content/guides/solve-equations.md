**SOLVE** finds a numerical value of **X** that makes an equation’s two sides agree. Try **2 × X + 3 = 11**:

1. Tap **AC**, then **2**, **×**.
2. Tap **ALPHA**, then **X** on the updated keypad.
3. Tap **+**, then **3**.
4. Tap **ALPHA**, then the **=** key directly below **SHIFT**, followed by **1**, **1**. This inserts the equation’s equals sign; the bottom-right **=** evaluates instead.
5. Tap **SHIFT**, then **SOLVE**.
6. At **X?**, enter **1** as a starting guess, then tap the bottom-right **=**.

The answer is **X = 4**. **L−R** is the difference between the left and right sides; a value close to zero means they agree closely. Here, it’s **0**.

SOLVE finds one numerical solution at a time. Tap **AC** to return to your equation for editing.

<details>
<summary>Try another solution</summary>

Some equations have several solutions. From a completed solution, tap **=** to solve again, enter a different starting guess at **X?**, then tap **=**.

A different guess may find another solution, but trying several guesses doesn’t guarantee you’ve found them all. The example above has just one solution.

</details>

<details>
<summary>If SOLVE can’t find a solution</summary>

Check your equation includes **X** and was entered as intended, then try another starting guess. **Can’t Solve** doesn’t necessarily mean there is no solution.

For trigonometric equations, check **Settings → Angle unit** matches your equation. If **Continue: =** appears, tap **=** to keep searching, or **AC** to leave.

</details>
