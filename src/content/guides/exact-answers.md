title: 'Exact answers and decimals'
description: 'Sumi shows supported exact answers automatically. See how to switch between fractions, square roots or multiples of π and their decimal values.'
publishedDate: 2026-09-10
order: 2
---

Enter your calculation and tap **=**. Sumi keeps answers as fractions, square roots or multiples of **π** where supported, without rounding them.

Try **√8**: tap **AC**, **√**, **8**, **=**. Sumi shows **2√2** automatically.

Tap **S⇔D** to see **2.828427125**, then tap it again to return to **2√2**. Press **=** before using **S⇔D**; the toggle works on the completed result, not the live preview while you type.

<details>
<summary>Why is my answer a decimal?</summary>

Some calculations only return decimals, and some fractions are too large to display exactly. **S⇔D** can only switch to an exact form the calculation supports.

The input matters too. Use **√2** rather than **2^0.5** for an exact square root, and **1 ÷ 3** rather than a rounded string of threes for one third. Sumi can’t recover the value you intended from a rounded input.

</details>
